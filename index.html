About Me

I am a Mechanical Engineer with over five years of professional experience across mechanical design, manufacturing engineering, project management and technical leadership in Australia and Colombia. Throughout my career, I have worked on a diverse range of engineering projects, from the development of mechanical and electromechanical products to industrial equipment, vertical transportation systems and manufacturing process improvements.

My professional background combines technical design expertise with practical experience in manufacturing, installation and project delivery. I have been involved in the complete engineering lifecycle, from understanding technical requirements and developing initial concepts to detailed design, fabrication, assembly, testing and commissioning. This experience has given me a strong appreciation of the relationship between engineering decisions, manufacturing capabilities, project constraints and the performance of the final product.

Mechanical Design and Manufacturing

I have developed advanced proficiency in SOLIDWORKS, using 3D modelling, complex assemblies, sheet metal design, weldments and parametric modelling to develop mechanical systems and production-ready engineering documentation. My approach to engineering design goes beyond creating functional models. I focus on understanding how components will be manufactured, assembled, installed and maintained, ensuring that technical solutions are both practical and economically viable.

Design for Manufacture and Assembly (DFMA) is a fundamental part of my engineering approach. I have applied these principles to simplify product designs, reduce manufacturing complexity, improve material utilisation and optimise fabrication processes. My experience includes working closely with production teams to improve designs for sheet metal fabrication, welding, machining and assembly, translating engineering concepts into reliable and cost-effective products.

Project Management and Engineering Leadership

Beyond technical design, I have experience coordinating engineering projects, managing manufacturing operations and leading technical teams. During my career, I have held responsibilities involving production planning, supplier coordination, resource management, project scheduling and technical problem-solving.

As a Production Manager, I led a team of 15 electromechanical technicians and coordinated activities across engineering, manufacturing, installation and maintenance. This experience strengthened my ability to balance technical requirements with operational priorities, manage competing demands and collaborate with people from different professional backgrounds.

My experience in industrial engineering projects has also involved coordinating contractors, supporting installation activities, monitoring project progress and resolving technical challenges during execution. These responsibilities have helped me develop a broader understanding of engineering project delivery, particularly the importance of communication, planning, safety and collaboration.

Engineering Processes and Continuous Improvement

I have a strong interest in improving not only the products being developed but also the engineering processes behind them. Throughout my career, I have contributed to initiatives involving design standardisation, manufacturing optimisation, engineering document management and workflow automation.

My experience includes implementing Product Data Management (PDM) systems, improving revision control and engineering documentation practices, and developing digital tools to support design automation and quality management. I enjoy identifying repetitive or inefficient processes and exploring how technology, standardisation and better engineering practices can improve productivity, traceability and consistency.

I believe effective engineering requires a combination of technical knowledge, analytical thinking, practical experience and a willingness to continuously question and improve existing solutions.

Professional Interests and Motivation

What motivates me most is the opportunity to solve meaningful engineering challenges, understand how complex systems work and contribute to projects that require collaboration across different disciplines.

I am particularly interested in expanding my involvement in larger and more technically complex engineering projects, where I can continue developing my capabilities in mechanical engineering, systems integration, project delivery and engineering leadership.

I value curiosity, adaptability, continuous learning and taking responsibility for the quality of my work. Having developed my career across different industries and countries, I have learned to approach unfamiliar challenges with an open mind, combining established engineering principles with practical problem-solving.

My professional goal is to continue growing as a well-rounded engineer, contributing not only through technical design but also through the coordination of people, processes and engineering activities that turn ideas into successful projects.
